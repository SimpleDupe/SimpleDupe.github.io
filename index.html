Quick setup

Up and running

Download the JAR and place it in your server's plugins folder.

Restart your Paper server, then edit plugins/SimpleDupe/config.yml to set your limits and blacklist.

Use /dupe [amount] to duplicate an item, or /simpledupereload to reload settings.

Requires Paper 1.21.1 and Java 21.
